The basics

A finished floor from the slab you already have.

Residential epoxy is a coating system applied directly to an interior concrete slab. After removing the old flooring and adhesive, we grind the concrete, fill cracks and tack strip holes, and build a base and color coat with a clear on top. The result has no grout lines, no seams and nothing for pet hair or dust to hide in.

Indoor floors give us more freedom with the look. Solid colors, light chip blends, metallics and satin or gloss clears are all on the table, and we can match a color to your cabinets, trim or tile in adjoining rooms.

Why choose it

Why homeowners choose it.

People usually come to us for one room, then ask about the rest of the house once they see how it cleans.

Spills stay on top

Laundry detergent, pet accidents and dropped drinks wipe up without soaking into grout or fibers.

Takes the weights

A high-build coating handles dumbbells, racks and rubber mats better than tile or laminate.

Less dust, fewer allergens

A sealed, seamless surface doesn’t trap dust or dander the way carpet and grout do.

Cool underfoot

Coated concrete stays cooler than carpet in summer, which is welcome in a West Valley July.

Options

Two common directions.

We bring large samples so you can see them in your room’s real light.

Practical

Solid color with satin clear

A uniform color with a lower-sheen clear looks calm, hides footprints and works in gyms, laundry rooms and hobby spaces. Add a fine anti-slip grit for wet areas.

Showpiece

Metallic or light flake

A metallic pour gives living areas and home bars depth and movement. A light, fine-chip blend reads clean and modern in casitas and mudrooms while forgiving a little dust.

Surprise notes

Casitas, next-gen suites and snowbird homes.

A lot of newer Surprise homes include a casita or a next-gen suite with its own entry, and many of those get used as gyms, studios or guest rooms. Older homes in the active-adult communities often have carpeted rooms that owners would rather not re-carpet. Both are good candidates for a coated slab.

Interior floors near sliding doors and big west windows still get real UV. In those rooms we use a non-yellowing clear so a white or light gray floor stays that color. If you’re away for part of the year, we can schedule the work while you’re gone and send photos at each stage.

Install days

What the days look like.

Interior jobs take more care around walls, doors and finished trim. Most single rooms take two days; larger areas take longer.

  1. Remove & protectOld flooring and adhesive come up, and we tape off walls, cabinets and doorways.
  2. Grind with dust controlWe grind with vacuums attached at the head and run air scrubbers in enclosed rooms.
  3. Fill & levelTack strip holes, cracks and low spots are filled and ground smooth.
  4. CoatBase, color and clear go down in sequence with the right recoat window between them.
  5. Hand backWe pull tape, clean up and give you the dates for furniture and rugs.

Pricing

What affects the cost.

Interior work is priced per job after a visit. These are the main drivers.

RemovalCarpet is quick to remove; tile and thinset take far longer to grind off.
Room layoutLots of corners, closets and doorways add hand-detailing time.
FinishMetallic floors take more labor and material than solid colors.
MoistureA slab that tests high for vapor may need a moisture-mitigating primer.

Care

Keeping it like new.

Dust-mop or vacuum with a hard-floor setting a few times a week, and damp-mop with a pH-neutral cleaner. Felt pads under chairs and table legs prevent scratches in the clear. Rubber gym mats are fine on a fully cured floor.

Avoid steam mops and harsh cleaners, and give the floor the full cure time on your card before moving heavy furniture back in.

Questions

Residential Epoxy Flooring: quick answers.

Can you coat over my existing tile?

We don’t recommend it. Tile can loosen and grout lines telegraph through. We remove the tile and thinset and coat the slab itself.

Is the floor cold or hard to stand on?

It feels like any hard floor. In a gym or work area we recommend rubber mats where you stand for long periods.

Will there be a strong smell?

Some products have an odor during application. We choose low-odor options for occupied homes and ventilate while we work.

Can you match my paint or cabinet color?

Often, yes. Solid-color systems can be tinted, and we’ll bring samples to compare in the room.

Does it work over radiant heat?

Yes, with the heat turned off during install and brought back up slowly afterward.

How soon can I move furniture back?

Light foot traffic is usually fine within a day. Furniture typically goes back a few days later; your cure card has the exact dates.
