Polyaspartic Floor Coatings in Surprise, AZ

Polyaspartic is the coating we reach for when a floor has to look good in the sun and be back in use quickly.

It’s a type of aliphatic polyurea that cures fast, resists UV and wears hard, which makes it ideal as a garage topcoat, a patio coating or a full system for businesses that can’t close for long.

The basics

Fast chemistry, carefully applied.

Polyaspartic coatings cure through a reaction that’s much quicker than standard epoxy. Depending on the product and temperature, a polyaspartic layer can be recoated in a few hours instead of overnight. That’s why a full chip floor can sometimes be installed in a single day.

Speed comes with a shorter working window, so installers have to move fast and keep a wet edge. It’s also aliphatic, meaning it won’t yellow under UV, which is why it’s our go-to clear for floors that see daylight.

Why choose it

What polyaspartic does well.

It’s not the answer for every layer, but for the top layer in Arizona it’s hard to beat.

Quick turnaround

Many floors are walkable the same day and ready for vehicles sooner than epoxy.

No yellowing

Stays clear and color-true where sun hits, including garage aprons and patios.

Tough surface

High abrasion and chemical resistance for tires, tools and foot traffic.

Wide temp range

Can be installed across a broad range of temperatures, with the right planning.

Options

Full system or topcoat?

We use polyaspartic in two main ways, depending on the slab and the schedule.

Full system

Polyaspartic from base to clear

For the fastest return to service, every layer is polyaspartic. Best on clean, sound slabs and for clients who need the space back quickly.

Hybrid

Epoxy or polyurea base, polyaspartic top

A slower base coat gets more time to soak into the concrete and bond, then the polyaspartic clear gives UV stability and wear resistance. Our most common garage build.

Surprise notes

Fast-cure coatings in a hot climate.

Heat speeds up polyaspartic even more. On a summer afternoon in Surprise, an unprepared crew can watch a batch start to set in the bucket. We handle that by starting at dawn, keeping materials shaded and cool, mixing smaller batches and choosing slower-reacting formulas for warm days.

The payoff is a topcoat that handles the West Valley sun. Open garage doors, south-facing patios and entries with afternoon glare keep their color and gloss with a polyaspartic clear.

What we plan around

Every polyaspartic job gets a temperature plan: start time, product grade and batch size are set the day before.

Install days

One-day installs, done right.

A typical polyaspartic garage system can be installed in one day; hybrid systems usually take two.

  1. Grind & repairSame thorough prep as any of our systems. Fast-cure coatings are less forgiving of poor prep, not more.
  2. BaseBase coat goes down in manageable sections with flake broadcast if chosen.
  3. ScrapeChips are scraped once the base is firm, often within an hour or two.
  4. ClearThe polyaspartic clear is applied the same day.
  5. ReturnWalkable within hours; vehicle times are listed on your cure card.

Pricing

What affects polyaspartic pricing.

Polyaspartic materials cost more than standard epoxy, but saving a day or two of downtime often makes up for it.

System typeA full polyaspartic system costs more than a hybrid with an epoxy base.
AreaLarger areas need more crew to keep a wet edge.
Prep needsOld coatings, sealers and damage add grinding and repair time.
FinishFlake, solid or clear-over-concrete all price differently.

Care

Simple care.

Sweep regularly, mop with a neutral cleaner, and wipe chemical spills promptly. Polyaspartic’s hard surface makes cleanup easy.

As with any coating, avoid dragging sharp metal objects and pad heavy point loads.

Questions

Polyaspartic Floor Coatings: quick answers.

Is polyaspartic better than epoxy?

It’s better as a topcoat in sun and for fast schedules. Epoxy is still excellent as a base and for thick interior builds. Many of our floors use both.

Can I really park the same day?

Usually not the same day. Foot traffic often is. Vehicle times depend on the product and temperature and are on your cure card.

Does it work outdoors?

Yes. It’s one of the best options for patios and entries because it resists UV.

Is polyaspartic the same as polyurea?

It’s a type of aliphatic polyurea with slower, more workable cure times and good UV resistance.

Does it smell?

Some polyaspartic products have a noticeable odor while curing. We ventilate and can choose lower-odor grades.

Can it go over my existing epoxy floor?

Sometimes, if the existing floor is well bonded. We’d test it and scuff-sand or grind before recoating.
