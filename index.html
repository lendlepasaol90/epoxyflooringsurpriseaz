Who we are

Bare slab in. Finished floor out.

Most concrete in Surprise was poured, troweled and left alone. It sheds dust, soaks up oil, and shows every tire mark and patch.

We're a Surprise floor coating crew that does one thing: build bonded resin floors on top of that concrete. Every job starts with a diamond grinder, not an acid wash, because a coating is only as strong as its grip on the slab. From there we fill cracks, build the color layer and finish with a clear chosen for how much sun, traffic and chemical exposure the floor will see.

You get a single point of contact, a written scope that names each product and coat, and a crew that cleans up before it leaves.

  • Written scope listing prep, repairs, each coat and the cure schedule
  • Dust-controlled grinding with HEPA vacuums attached to the grinder
  • Residential and commercial crews, scheduled around heat and your hours

Local conditions

Built for the far West Valley.

A coating that works in a mild climate can fail here in a single summer. These are the four things we plan every Surprise floor around.

01

Slab heat

Summer garage slabs get hot enough to shorten working time and soften weak coatings. We schedule early starts and pick products rated for hot-tire contact.

02

Monsoon dust

Haboobs and field dust blow fine grit under every garage door. Textured chip finishes hide it, and a hard clear stops it from scratching the color.

03

Shifting soils

Clay-rich soils in parts of the valley move with moisture, so hairline cracks are common. We rout and fill them and use flexible bases where movement is likely.

04

Carts & RVs

Golf carts, RVs, boats and toy haulers are part of life here. Their weight, tires and drips call for a thicker build than a basic car garage.

Process

From measure to move-in.

Six steps, the same on every job. Most garages go from bare concrete to finished floor in one or two working days.

Measure

We visit, check moisture and hardness, note cracks and coatings, and show samples in your light.

Scope

You get a written quote naming each coat, the product, the repair plan and the cure times.

Grind

Diamond grinding opens the surface to a profile the coating can lock into, with dust captured at the head.

Repair

Cracks are routed and filled, divots patched, and joints filled or kept open to suit the slab.

Build

Base coat, color or chip broadcast, scrape and vacuum, then the clear topcoat that takes the wear.

Cure

We walk the floor with you and leave a cure card with dates for foot traffic, storage and vehicles.

Why us

The details that keep floors bonded.

Coating failures almost always trace back to prep, product choice or rushing the cure. These are the habits that prevent them.

Grind, never etch

Acid etching leaves residue and an uneven profile. Mechanical grinding gives a clean, consistent surface on every square foot.

Moisture checked

Vapor pushing up through a slab can lift a coating from below. We test before we quote, not after it bubbles.

Matched chemistry

We combine epoxy, polyurea and polyaspartic where each works best rather than selling one product for every room.

Heat-aware scheduling

In summer we start at sunrise and mix smaller batches so fast-cure products level out before the slab heats up.

Plain-language quotes

Every line on the scope says what it is and why it's there. No surprise add-ons halfway through the job.

Snowbird friendly

Away for part of the year? We can coordinate access, send photos of each stage and finish before you're back.

DIY kits vs. professional

Why big-box kits peel in Surprise garages.

Store kits are thin, water-based epoxy that's meant to go over acid-etched concrete. In a mild climate they can last a while. Here, a hot tire parked on a thin film after a summer drive softens it, grabs it and pulls it off the slab. That's the classic "hot tire pickup" peel.

A professional system is several times thicker, uses 100% solids or fast-cure resins, and goes onto a ground surface the coating can physically key into.

Typical kit

  • Acid etch prep, often uneven
  • One thin coat, a few mils thick
  • Cracks painted over, not repaired
  • No UV-stable clear coat

Our system

  • Diamond-ground profile
  • Multi-coat build, many times thicker
  • Cracks routed and filled first
  • UV-stable, hot-tire-rated topcoat

Floor care

Easy upkeep, long life.

A coated floor needs far less attention than bare concrete. A handful of habits keeps it sharp for years to come.

Sweep the grit

Dust from monsoon storms acts like sandpaper underfoot. A weekly pass with a soft broom or dust mop keeps the gloss sharp.

Mop pH-neutral

Warm water and a neutral cleaner are all it needs. Skip vinegar, citrus degreasers and bleach, which can dull the clear.

Wipe spills soon

Oil, brake fluid, pool chemicals and fertilizer won't stain right away, but they shouldn't sit for days.

Pad sharp loads

Put plywood or pads under kickstands, jack stands, trailer tongues and heavy shelving feet.

Mats at the door

A walk-off mat at the house door and the garage entry catches grit before it spreads.

Respect the cure

Stick to the dates on your cure card before the car, the cart or heavy shelving goes back in. Rushing it is the easiest way to mark a new floor.

FAQ

Straight answers.

The questions Surprise homeowners and business owners ask most before booking a floor.

Ask Us Directly
How much does an epoxy garage floor cost in Surprise?

It depends on square footage, the condition of the concrete, how much crack repair is needed and which system you choose. A chip floor with a polyaspartic clear costs more than a single solid coat but lasts far longer. We measure in person and give you a written, line-item quote before any work starts.

Will the coating hold up to Arizona heat?

Yes, when it's the right system. We use hot-tire-rated base coats and UV-stable polyaspartic or aliphatic clears for garages and patios, and we install during the cooler part of the day in summer so the products level and bond properly.

How long does installation take?

Most residential garages take one to two days. Larger homes, metallic floors and commercial spaces take longer, and we phase big jobs so part of the building stays usable.

When can I park in the garage again?

Fast-cure systems often allow light foot traffic the same evening and vehicles within a few days. Epoxy-based systems need longer. Your cure card lists the exact dates for your products and the temperature on install day.

Do you coat over existing paint or sealer?

No. We grind off old paint, sealer and failing coatings so the new system bonds to clean concrete. Coating over a weak layer only moves the failure point.

Is a coated floor slippery?

Chip floors have a natural texture. For smoother floors, patios and areas near doors, we add a fine anti-slip aggregate to the clear coat.

Can you fix cracks before coating?

Yes. Cracks are cut open, cleaned and filled with a rigid or flexible repair material, then ground flush. Moving cracks may still telegraph through over time, and we'll tell you up front if a crack looks active.

Do you work in Sun City Grand and other HOA communities?

Yes. We regularly work in active-adult and HOA neighborhoods in Surprise and can schedule around community rules for contractor hours and parking.
